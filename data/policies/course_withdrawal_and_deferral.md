# Course Interruption, Deferral, and Withdrawal Policy 2026

**Document Code**: REG-2026-INT  
**Authoritative Body**: University Registry & Student Records Directorate  
**Effective Date**: Academic Year 2026/2027  

---

### §1.0 Authorized Interruption of Studies (Leave of Absence)
Students experiencing acute medical, emotional, or personal crises that render continuing study unfeasible may apply for an **Authorized Interruption of Studies**:
* **Standard Duration**: Granted for periods of **one term up to a maximum of twelve (12) consecutive months**.
* **Preservation of Academic Standing**: Modules successfully passed prior to interruption remain credited on the student's transcript without expiry.
* **Campus Facilities Access**: During an authorized interruption, students retain library borrowing privileges, student email access, and university counseling services access for 60 days following the start of leave.

---

### §2.0 Module Assessment Deferral
* Students facing acute medical or personal emergencies during exam periods may apply to **defer assessment** without penalty.
* Deferred assessments are taken as a **first attempt ('uncapped')** during the next scheduled examination period (typically the August resit window).

---

### §3.0 Census Dates and Tuition Fee Liability
Students considering complete withdrawal or interruption must observe the official institutional census dates governing tuition fee obligations:
1. **Withdrawal before End of Week 3 (Term 1)**: 0% tuition fee liability. Full refund of tuition paid.
2. **Withdrawal during Weeks 4 through 11**: **25% tuition fee liability** for the academic year.
3. **Withdrawal between Start of Term 2 and Week 20**: **50% tuition fee liability**.
4. **Withdrawal after Week 20**: **100% full tuition fee liability**.

---

### §4.0 Medical Fitness to Return Protocol
* Where an interruption was granted on medical or psychiatric grounds, the student must provide a **Medical Fit to Return Certificate** from their treating physician or psychiatrist at least **30 calendar days prior** to the proposed resumption date.
* The University Occupational Health & Wellbeing Service reviews the certification to ensure adequate academic adjustments are in place before enrollment reactivation.
