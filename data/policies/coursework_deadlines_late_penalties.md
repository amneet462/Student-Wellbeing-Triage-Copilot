# Assessment Submission Deadlines and Late Penalty Regulations 2026

**Document Code**: REG-2026-SUB  
**Authoritative Body**: University Assessment Standards & Quality Committee  
**Effective Date**: Academic Year 2026/2027  

---

### §1.0 Assessment Submission Timings
* **Standard Deadline**: All digital coursework assignments must be uploaded through the university Virtual Learning Environment (VLE) by **12:00:00 noon (UK Local Time)** on the specified submission date.
* **Timestamp Verification**: The authoritative submission time is recorded automatically by the digital submission portal server, not the student's local computer clock.

---

### §2.0 Late Submission Penalty Tariff
Unless a formal extension has been granted under Policy REG-2026-EC, any coursework submitted after the 12:00 noon deadline is subject to automatic percentage mark deductions:

1. **Within 24 Hours Late (Up to 12:00 noon next day)**:
   * A fixed deduction of **5 percentage points** (e.g., a raw mark of 68% is reduced to 63%).
2. **24 to 48 Hours Late**:
   * A fixed deduction of **10 percentage points** (e.g., 68% becomes 58%).
3. **48 to 72 Hours Late**:
   * A fixed deduction of **15 percentage points**.
4. **Each Subsequent 24-Hour Period**:
   * An additional **5 percentage points per day** deducted until the maximum 7-day cutoff is reached.
5. **Pass Mark Floor**: Deductions will not reduce a passing mark below the baseline pass mark (40% for undergraduate, 50% for postgraduate) unless the submission falls under §3.0.

---

### §2.1 Weekend and Public Holiday Calculations
* For the purpose of late penalty calculations, **Saturday and Sunday collectively count as one single 24-hour period**.
* Submitting coursework between 12:01 PM on Friday and 12:00 noon on Monday incurs a single 24-hour penalty deduction (5 percentage points).

---

### §3.0 Hard Cutoff Limit (7 Calendar Days)
* Coursework submitted **more than seven (7) calendar days** after the published deadline will automatically receive a mark of **zero percent (0%)**.
* The assessment will be recorded as a non-submission (`NS`), requiring a reassessment opportunity during the summer examination board period.

---

### §4.0 Technical and Network Failure Protocol
* If a student encounters a technical failure with the university portal during submission, the student must notify the IT Helpdesk and their Module Leader **within one (1) hour of the deadline**.
* The student must attach the unedited coursework file and a full-screen screenshot showing the server error and system timestamp.
