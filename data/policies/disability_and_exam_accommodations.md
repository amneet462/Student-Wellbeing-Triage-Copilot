# Disability Inclusion and Examination Accommodations Framework 2026

**Document Code**: DIS-2026-ACC  
**Authoritative Body**: University Disability, Neurodiversity & Inclusion Service  
**Effective Date**: Academic Year 2026/2027  

---

### §1.0 Scope and Protected Conditions
The university is legally committed to providing reasonable academic adjustments to ensure equal access for students with:
* Mental health conditions (e.g. chronic anxiety, clinical depression, bipolar disorder, PTSD).
* Neurodivergence (e.g. ADHD, Autism Spectrum Condition, Dyslexia, Dyspraxia).
* Long-term chronic medical conditions and physical or sensory impairments.

---

### §2.0 Summary of Adjustments (SoA) and Learning Plans
* Upon registration with Disability Services, a dedicated Disability Advisor conducts an intake assessment and establishes an official **Summary of Adjustments (SoA)**.
* Once published, the SoA is distributed securely to faculty and examination boards, removing the need for students to repeatedly explain their medical history to individual professors.

---

### §3.0 Standard Examination Accommodations
Students with approved mental health or neurodivergent profiles on their SoA are entitled to the following standard exam adjustments:
1. **Extra Writing Time**: Standard provision of **25% additional time** (15 minutes per hour) to accommodate processing differences and panic mitigation.
2. **Rest and Movement Breaks**: **10 minutes of supervised rest break per exam hour**, during which the exam clock stops.
3. **Low-Distraction Venue**: Allocation to a quiet, reduced-capacity room (maximum 15 candidates) rather than a high-stress main sports hall.
4. **Ergonomic & Assistive Provisions**: Adjustable height desks, specialized noise-cancelling headphones, and permission to bring prescribed anxiety medication or sensory grounding objects into the examination room.

---

### §4.0 Deadlines for Examination Provision Registration
* To guarantee exam accommodations for the Semester 1 Examination Window, registration and documentation must be finalized by **Friday of Teaching Week 8**.
* Late registrations received after Week 8 will be applied where operationally possible; otherwise, students may defer to the resit period without academic penalty.
