# Student Hardship Fund & Emergency Financial Assistance Regulations 2026

**Document Code**: FIN-2026-AID  
**Authoritative Body**: Student Financial Support Services & University Treasury  
**Effective Date**: Academic Year 2026/2027  

---

### §1.0 Purpose and Eligibility of Hardship Funds
The **University Student Hardship Fund (USHF)** provides discretionary non-repayable financial support to students who encounter unexpected, acute financial emergencies that jeopardize their continuation at university.
* **Eligible Cohorts**: Registered full-time and part-time undergraduate, postgraduate taught, and doctoral students (including domestic, EU, and international students).
* **Requirement**: The student must demonstrate that they had made reasonable financial provisions prior to starting the academic year, and that the financial shortfall arose from unforeseen adversity.

---

### §2.0 Available Grant Categories and Grant Caps
1. **Emergency Living Crisis Grant**:
   * Designed for food insecurity, unexpected utility cutoffs, or emergency medical expenditures.
   * Awarded up to **£1,500 per academic year** (non-repayable).
2. **Emergency Rent and Homelessness Prevention Grant**:
   * Designed to prevent immediate eviction or secure emergency student accommodation.
   * Awarded up to **£2,000 per claim** directly to student housing or accredited landlords.
3. **Digital Hardship & Equipment Voucher**:
   * Provision of a loaned laptop or digital equipment voucher up to **£400** where primary study equipment is lost, damaged, or unaffordable.

---

### §3.0 24-Hour Short-Term Emergency Bridging Loan
* For students awaiting delayed government student maintenance loans or scholarships, the university provides an immediate interest-free **Emergency Bridging Loan of up to £350**.
* **Processing Speed**: Approved within **24 business hours** upon submission of proof of bank account and university student card.
* **Repayment**: Automatically deducted when the delayed loan installment arrives, with 0% interest and no administration fee.

---

### §4.0 Application and Evidence Submission
* Applications are submitted online through the Student Financial Aid Portal.
* Required evidence: Three (3) consecutive months of bank statements for all active accounts, a copy of the student tenancy agreement, and a breakdown of projected income vs essential living expenses.
