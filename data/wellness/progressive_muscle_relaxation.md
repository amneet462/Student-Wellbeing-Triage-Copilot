# Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR) for Somatic Tension Release

**Clinical Framework**: Jacobson Progressive Relaxation Protocol & Somatic De-escalation  
**Target Symptoms**: Somatic exam tension, clenched jaw, tension headaches, physical restlessness  
**Estimated Time**: 8 to 12 minutes  

---

### §1.0 The Mind-Body Muscular Tension Loop
Stress is not solely mental; it expresses physically through chronic skeletal muscle contraction. During revision and examination crunch periods, students unconsciously clench their jaws, hike their shoulders toward their ears, and constrict abdominal muscles. This muscular tightness feeds continuous somatic alarm signals back to the brainstem.

**Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR)**, developed by Dr. Edmund Jacobson, operates on a proven physiological law: **a muscle group that is deliberately contracted to maximal tension for 5 seconds will reflexively rebound into a state of deeper physiological relaxation** than it held before.

---

### §2.0 Step-by-Step Somatic Sequence
Sit in a comfortable armchair or lie flat on your back. Loosen tight clothing and remove spectacles. Inhale as you tense; exhale with a release sound as you let go.

1. **FEET & TOES**:
   * Curl your toes tightly downwards into a ball for **5 seconds**. Feel the tension in the arches.
   * Release abruptly. Notice the sudden sensation of warmth and lightness for **15 seconds**.
2. **CALVES & LOWER LEGS**:
   * Pull your toes upward toward your shins, flexing your calf muscles tightly for **5 seconds**.
   * Release completely. Let your legs go limp like heavy lead.
3. **THIGHS & GLUTES**:
   * Squeeze your quadriceps and glutes together firmly for **5 seconds**.
   * Release and breathe out. Feel your hips sink down into the chair.
4. **ABDOMEN & CORE**:
   * Tighten your stomach muscles as if bracing for an impact for **5 seconds**.
   * Release. Allow your belly to rise softly and naturally with each breath.
5. **HANDS & FOREARMS**:
   * Clench both hands into tight fists, drawing your knuckles toward your forearms for **5 seconds**.
   * Open your fingers wide and let your palms rest completely open and soft.
6. **SHOULDERS & NECK (High Stress Area)**:
   * Shrug your shoulders all the way up to your ears, clenching your trapezius muscles for **5 seconds**.
   * Drop your shoulders down instantly. Feel the tension draining down through your arms.
7. **JAW & FACIAL MUSCLES**:
   * Clench your teeth gently and scrunch your eyes, nose, and forehead into a tight grimace for **5 seconds**.
   * Release all facial expression. Part your teeth slightly so your jaw hangs completely loose.

---

### §3.0 30-Second Desk Rapid Release for Revision
When studying in the library:
* Simply do Step 5 (Hands) and Step 6 (Shoulders) together.
* Take one deep breath, shrug your shoulders to your ears, clench your fists for 5 seconds, and drop them with a long exhale.
